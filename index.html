Ready for India's digital health network

AGAAEE is being built ABDM-first, on the current V3 APIs, across all three integration milestones: ABHA creation and verification (M1) with Scan & Share at the front desk, sharing digital health records as a Health Information Provider (M2) with FHIR records and consent-based exchange, and consented record retrieval as a Health Information User (M3) — targeting certification as a full Health Repository Provider.

Built with owner-doctors, not just for them

AGAAEE is made for the hospitals that carry most of India's private care — the 10–50-bed nursing home where the owner is also the busiest doctor.

  • Patient registration with ABHA at the front desk
  • Consultations documented in under a minute, e-prescriptions included
  • Pharmacy with batch, expiry and schedule-drug registers handled
  • Bills, GST and scheme claims from the same screen
  • Lab reports and discharge summaries, shareable as digital health records
  • Works on the computers and connections you already have
