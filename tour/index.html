Every page below is a real screen from the working software, exported straight from the application. Nothing here is a mock-up or an illustration.

Sample data only. Every patient, doctor and prescription shown is fabricated for demonstration. The connection to India's national health network (ABDM) is simulated while our sandbox registration is being approved — the screens themselves are the real ones.
